Frequently asked questions. What does Nexora build? Nexora designs and builds digital experiences and frontend-focused business solutions, including websites, landing pages, customer journeys, digital workflows, integrations with existing tools, SEO foundations, analytics setup and optimisation. Who is Nexora built for? Businesses that want to improve how they present themselves online, attract and guide customers, and organise the processes behind their digital presence.

Do I need to know exactly what I need before contacting Nexora? No. You can come to us with a business problem rather than a predefined technical solution. We will first understand what you are trying to achieve and then determine what should be built. Can Nexora work with my existing website? Yes. If your existing website has a useful foundation, we can improve, restructure or extend it rather than automatically starting from zero.

Does Nexora build backend systems and payment integrations? Not currently. Our current focus is frontend development and digital experiences using technologies such as HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Backend development, custom payment processing and complex server-side integrations are outside our current service scope.

How long does a project take? It depends on the scope. A focused website or landing page can move quickly, while a larger digital experience requires more planning, design, development and testing. We will establish the expected timeline before development begins. How much does a project cost? Pricing depends on the scope, complexity and requirements of the project. After understanding what needs to be built, we will provide a clear scope and proposal before work begins.

What happens after the website launches? Launch is not necessarily the end. Depending on the project, Nexora can continue helping with improvements, SEO, performance, analytics and conversion optimisation as the business grows.

Have a project in mind? Tell us what you are trying to achieve, where you are stuck, or what you want to build. We will help you identify the right digital direction and the next step.
